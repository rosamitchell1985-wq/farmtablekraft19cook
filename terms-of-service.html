4. Reservations

A reservation request submitted through this site is a request, not a confirmed booking. A booking exists only once a member of our team confirms it by email, text message or telephone.

  • Standard tables. Free, no card held, cancel at any time without charge.
  • Garden Table. A $25 deposit is taken at confirmation and credited to your check on the night. Refundable if cancelled more than 24 hours before the booking; retained if cancelled inside 24 hours.
  • Chef's Table tasting. Charged in full at $145 per guest at booking. Refundable if cancelled more than 72 hours before. Inside 72 hours we will move the booking once, free, to any date within three months, and will refund you if we manage to resell the seats.

We hold tables for 15 minutes past the booking time unless you tell us you are delayed. We may cancel a booking and refund any deposit in full if a restaurant closes for reasons outside our control, including severe weather, utility failure or a public health order.

5. Orders, takeout and delivery

An order placed here is an offer to buy, which we accept when we confirm it. We may decline an order if an item has sold out, if the delivery address is outside our five mile radius, or if we cannot safely meet a stated allergen requirement.

Our own delivery covers five miles from each restaurant at a flat $4.95. Delivery times given are estimates in good faith and are not guarantees. If food arrives in unacceptable condition, contact the restaurant that day and we will remake it or refund it. Alcohol is only handed over to a person aged 21 or over who can produce photo identification.

6. Gift cards

Gift cards are issued in values from $25 to $500, have no expiry date, and carry no activation, dormancy, inactivity or replacement fee. They may be used at all three restaurants, for first-party online orders, for event invoices and on the pantry shelf. They cannot be used on third-party delivery apps. They are not reloadable and cannot be redeemed for cash except for balances under $10 where state law permits, which currently includes North Carolina, South Carolina and Tennessee. Lost cards are replaced once, free, on production of the purchase confirmation.

7. Events and catering

Where the contract and these terms conflict, the contract governs. Private room and buyout bookings take a 25 percent deposit, fully refundable more than 30 days before the event, half refundable to 14 days, and non-refundable inside 14 days because food and labour are committed by then. Final guest numbers are due seven days ahead and are the number you are charged for.

8. Twelve Mile Club rewards

Membership is free and open to anyone aged 18 or over. Points accrue at one point per dollar spent on food and drink, excluding tax, gratuity, gift card purchases and orders placed on third-party apps. Two hundred and fifty points converts to a $25 credit. Points and credits have no cash value, cannot be sold or transferred, and are void if obtained by fraud. We may change the program with 30 days notice posted on this site, and any credit already earned will be honoured under the terms in force when it was earned.
