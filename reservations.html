Booking and cancellation policy

Short version: we do not charge you for changing your mind, as long as you tell us in time.

No. Standard tables are booked on trust. If your plans change, cancel online or call us, and there is no charge at any point. We ask only that you tell us, because an empty held table is a table a walk-in guest could have used.

The $25 is taken when a host confirms your booking and appears as a credit line on your check that night, so the table effectively costs nothing extra. Cancel more than 24 hours ahead and we refund it to the original card within five business days. Cancel inside 24 hours and we keep it, because that window is too short to fill a six-top.

The full $145 per guest is charged at booking because the menu is bought in for a fixed count. Cancel more than 72 hours out and you get every dollar back. Inside 72 hours we will move your booking once, free, to any date within three months. If you simply cannot come, we will still try to resell the seats and refund you if we succeed.

We hold your table for 15 minutes past your booking time, and longer if you call to say you are running behind. Beyond that, the table may be released to waiting guests. Repeated no-shows on premium tiers may mean we ask for a deposit on future bookings.

Nine guests or more is handled by our events team rather than the reservation book. The private dining room seats 10 to 34 with a $500 room fee and a food and drink minimum that varies by night. Full restaurant buyouts are possible on Mondays. See private events and catering for package pricing.

Children are welcome at every service and we keep high chairs, booster seats and a genuinely good children's plate at $9. Dogs are welcome on the Charleston courtyard and the Asheville sidewalk tables. All three dining rooms have step-free entry and accessible restrooms. Tell us in the notes field and we will seat you accordingly.
