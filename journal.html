What gets written here

We publish when there is something worth saying, which works out at roughly one piece every six weeks. Subjects are always one of four things: how we buy, how we cook, what something actually costs, or a mistake we made and what it taught us.

There is no newsletter signup wall and no download gate. If you want to know when something new goes up, the simplest thing is to check the journal every couple of months, or ask a server, because the whole team reads these before they publish.
