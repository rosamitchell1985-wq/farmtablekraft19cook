Takeout and delivery

Order here and you pay menu price. No service fee, no packaging fee, no small-order fee. Our own drivers cover five miles from each door for a flat $4.95, and we are honest below about what the third-party apps cost you instead.

Pickup

Menu price, nothing added

Choose a 15 minute pickup window. Park in the two reserved bays outside each restaurant and we bring the bag out to your car if you would rather not come in.

Fee: $0.00

Our delivery

Flat $4.95 inside five miles

Driven by our own staff in insulated boxes, so the food arrives at the temperature it left at. Tips go 100 percent to the driver and are entirely optional.

Fee: $4.95 flat, no distance surcharge

Delivery apps

Available, but pricier

We list on the major apps because some guests prefer them. Their commission is between 15 and 30 percent, so their menu prices are marked up to cover it. We would rather you ordered here.

Fee: set by the app, not by us

Set quantities and the estimate updates as you go. Sales tax is shown at the North Carolina prepared-food rate of 7.25 percent, and adjusts to your state at checkout.

Ordering questions

About 25 minutes for most orders placed outside peak service. Between 6:30 PM and 8:00 PM on a Friday or Saturday it can stretch to 45 minutes, and we will tell you that in the confirmation text rather than let you find out in the parking lot. Anything with the whole roast chicken or the hog shoulder needs the notice periods listed on the menu.

The tomato toast goes soft within ten minutes, the skillet cornbread loses its crust, and the shellfish pot is genuinely not worth ordering to go. We will say so when you order rather than take your money for a dish that arrives worse than it left. Everything from the hearth section, the grits and all four sweets travel well.

Yes. Twelve Mile Club points accrue on takeout and delivery at the same rate as dine-in, one point per dollar of food and drink, and a $25 credit unlocks at 250 points. Points do not accrue on orders placed through third-party apps, because we never see your account details on those platforms.

Beer, cider and wine can be added to delivery orders in North Carolina and Tennessee where our permits allow it, and the driver will check government-issued photo identification at the door. If nobody aged 21 or over is there to sign, the alcohol goes back to the restaurant and is refunded. Cocktails are not available to go.
