Privacy Policy

Last updated August 12, 2026. This policy explains what personal information Farm Table Kraft LLC collects through this website and in our restaurants, why we collect it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • We collect what we need to take your booking, fill your order and answer your message. Nothing else.
  • We do not sell personal information to data brokers, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising unless you consent.
  • Non-essential cookies, including Google advertising cookies, are set only after you agree through the consent banner.
  • You can ask us what we hold, ask for a copy, or ask us to delete it, at privacy@farmtablekraft.com.

1. Who we are

This website is operated by Farm Table Kraft LLC, a limited liability company registered in the State of North Carolina, with its registered office and mailing address at 42 Biltmore Avenue, Asheville, NC 28801, United States. For the purposes of this policy, Farm Table Kraft LLC is the business responsible for the personal information described here.

You can reach our privacy contact by email at privacy@farmtablekraft.com, by phone on +1 (828) 555-0142, or by writing to the postal address above marked for the attention of the Privacy Contact.

2. What personal information we collect

We collect the following categories of information. Nothing on this list is collected speculatively, and each item has a stated purpose.

Information you give us directly

  • Reservation details. Name, email address, phone number, party size, date and time, occasion, and any dietary or accessibility notes you choose to add.
  • Order details. Name, phone number, delivery address where relevant, the items ordered and any preparation notes.
  • Event and catering enquiries. Name, organisation, contact details, event date, guest count, budget range if you choose to give it, and the description you write.
  • Gift card purchases. Purchaser and recipient names and email addresses, delivery ZIP code for physical cards, and the message you write on the card.
  • Job applications. Name, contact details, the role applied for, any link you supply, and the text of your application.
  • Contact messages. Whatever you write to us, plus the contact details you give so we can reply.
  • Rewards enrolment. Name, email address, phone number and your transaction history with us, used to calculate Twelve Mile Club points.

Information collected automatically

  • Strictly necessary technical data. Your IP address, browser type and version, device type, and the pages you request. This is processed to deliver the site securely and to detect abuse. It is collected regardless of consent because the site cannot function without it.
  • Analytics data, only with consent. Aggregate measurements such as pages viewed, time on page, referring website and approximate region. This is set only if you agree to analytics cookies.
  • Advertising data, only with consent. Identifiers used by advertising vendors, including Google, to select and measure advertisements. This is set only if you agree to advertising cookies.

Payment information

We do not store full payment card numbers on this website or on our own systems. Card payments are processed by our payment provider, which holds the card data under its own contractual and PCI DSS obligations. We receive only a transaction reference, the last four digits, the card brand and the outcome.

3. Why we use it

  • To take, confirm, change and honour reservations, orders, event bookings and gift card purchases.
  • To reply to messages, enquiries and job applications.
  • To handle allergen and accessibility requirements safely.
  • To operate the Twelve Mile Club rewards program, including calculating and applying credits.
  • To keep records we are required to keep for tax, accounting and employment purposes.
  • To secure the website, prevent fraud and investigate misuse.
  • With your consent, to measure how the website is used and to show and measure advertising.

4. Cookies and similar technologies

A cookie is a small text file stored on your device by a website. We also use similar technologies such as local storage. Our use falls into three categories.

  1. Strictly necessary. Required for the site to work: session security, load balancing, remembering your cookie choice, and holding the contents of an order form while you complete it. These are set without consent because the service cannot be provided without them.
  2. Analytics. Aggregate measurement of traffic and page performance. Set only after you agree.
  3. Advertising. Used by third-party advertising vendors, including Google, to select advertisements, limit how often you see the same advertisement, and measure whether an advertisement led to a visit. Set only after you agree.

You are asked to choose the first time you visit. Nothing beyond strictly necessary storage is written before you make that choice. You can change your decision at any time using the link in the footer of every page. Full detail of each cookie is in our Cookie Policy.

6. When we share information

We share personal information only in these situations: with the service providers listed above, acting on our instructions; where you have asked us to (for example, sending a gift card to a recipient you name); where required by law, court order or a lawful request from a public authority; and in connection with a sale or reorganisation of the business, in which case the recipient would be bound by this policy until it published its own.

We do not sell personal information to data brokers. We do not provide guest lists to marketing companies.

8. California residents: CCPA and CPRA

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act gives you the following rights.

  • Right to know. To request the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected about you, the sources, the purposes, and the categories of third parties we disclosed it to.
  • Right to delete. To request deletion of personal information we collected from you, subject to exceptions such as completing a transaction or meeting a legal obligation.
  • Right to correct. To request correction of inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to opt out of sale or sharing. To direct us not to sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.
  • Right to limit use of sensitive personal information. We do not collect sensitive personal information as that term is defined, other than dietary or accessibility notes you volunteer, which we use only to serve you safely.
  • Right against retaliation. We will not deny you service, charge you a different price or give you a lower quality of service because you exercised any of these rights.

Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information. We do not sell personal information for money. Advertising cookies may, under California law, constitute "sharing" for cross-context behavioural advertising. If you have consented to advertising cookies and wish to withdraw that consent, use the link in our footer and switch advertising off, or email privacy@farmtablekraft.com with the subject line "Do Not Sell or Share". Both routes have the same effect and neither requires you to create an account.

To make any request, email privacy@farmtablekraft.com or call +1 (828) 555-0142. We will verify your identity using information already in our records, respond within 45 days, and may extend once by a further 45 days where the request is complex, telling you before we do. An authorised agent may act on your behalf with written permission.

9. Visitors from the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom

Our restaurants are in the United States and this website is aimed at guests in the United States. If you visit from the EEA, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the General Data Protection Regulation and equivalent UK law may still apply to the information we collect from you. Where it does:

  • We rely on contract to process reservations, orders and event bookings; on legitimate interests to answer enquiries, secure the site and keep proper business records; on consent for analytics and advertising cookies; and on legal obligation for tax and employment records.
  • You have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, and the right to withdraw consent at any time without affecting processing already carried out.
  • Information you give us is transferred to and stored in the United States. Where a transfer requires a safeguard, we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses with our processors.
  • You may lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority, or with the UK Information Commissioner's Office if you are in the United Kingdom.

10. Children's privacy

This website is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13 years of age. In line with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act, we do not permit users under 13 to submit any form on this site, create a rewards account or make a purchase.

Children are of course welcome to eat in our restaurants. When a parent or guardian makes a booking that includes a child, we record only what is needed to seat and feed that child safely, such as a high chair requirement or an allergy, and that information is attached to the adult's booking rather than to a record about the child.

If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, email privacy@farmtablekraft.com and we will delete it promptly.

11. How we protect information

The site is served over HTTPS. Access to guest records is limited to staff who need it for their role and is logged. Payment card data never reaches our systems. Paper reservation sheets and allergy notes are destroyed at the end of each week. We test our forms and hosting configuration when we change them.

No system is perfectly secure, and we will not claim otherwise. If a breach occurs that is likely to result in risk to you, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by applicable state law.

12. Your choices in one place

  • Cookies. Use the link in the footer at any time.
  • Marketing email. We send transactional email by default and marketing email only if you opt in. Every marketing email has a one-click unsubscribe.
  • Rewards. Ask any host stand to close your Twelve Mile Club account. Any unspent credit is honoured for 12 months after closure.
  • Browser controls. Most browsers let you block or delete cookies, and we honour Global Privacy Control signals as an opt-out of sharing where your browser sends one.

13. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy we update the date at the top of the page. If a change materially affects how we use information you have already given us, we will say so on the home page for at least 30 days and, where the change requires it, ask for your consent again.
