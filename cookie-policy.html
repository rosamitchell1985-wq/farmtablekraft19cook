Cookie Policy

Last updated August 12, 2026. This page lists every cookie and storage item this website can set, what each one does, how long it lasts, and how to change your mind at any point.

Change your choice right now

Open the consent panel and switch analytics or advertising on or off. Your decision applies immediately.

1. What a cookie is

A cookie is a small text file that a website asks your browser to store. It can be read back later by the site that set it, or in the case of a third-party cookie, by another company whose code runs on the page. We also use local storage, which works similarly but is not sent with every request. Throughout this policy, "cookie" covers both.

2. Our consent rule

Before you make a choice, this site sets nothing except strictly necessary storage. No analytics cookie and no advertising cookie is written until you press Accept all, or until you switch that category on in Manage preferences and save. Pressing Reject non-essential means no analytics or advertising cookie is ever set on this device unless you later change it yourself.

Your choice is stored in local storage under the key ftk_consent_v1 so that we do not ask you again on every page. That single item is itself strictly necessary, because without it we would have no record of what you decided.

3. Strictly necessary

Always active. The site cannot function without these and there is no opt out, though you can block them in your browser settings at the cost of breaking bookings and orders.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve advertisements based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings, at aboutads.info, or at the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out. Declining advertising in our banner stops them being set here in the first place, which is the simpler route.

6. Third-party content we deliberately avoid

This site does not embed social media widgets, comment systems, chat popups, session recorders or heat mapping tools. Fonts are requested from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address as part of serving the file but does not set a cookie for that request. Photographs are requested from image hosts, which likewise receive your IP address as part of delivering the image.

7. Controlling cookies in your browser

Every major browser lets you see, block and delete cookies. Blocking all cookies will break reservations and ordering on this site, and on most others. Useful settings pages:

  • Chrome: Settings, then Privacy and security, then Third-party cookies
  • Safari: Settings, then Privacy
  • Firefox: Settings, then Privacy and Security
  • Edge: Settings, then Cookies and site permissions

We honour the Global Privacy Control signal. If your browser or extension sends one, we treat it as an instruction to switch advertising off, in addition to whatever you chose in our banner.

8. Changes

If we add a vendor or a cookie, this table changes and the date at the top updates. Where a new category is added, we will ask for consent again rather than assume your previous answer covers it.
