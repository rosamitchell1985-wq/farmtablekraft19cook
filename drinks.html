Drinks and bar menu

Twenty pours across five sections. The cocktail list moves with the orchard and the herb bed, the wine list stays short on purpose, and the zero proof section gets the same attention as everything else.

Bar pick Chosen by our bar lead, Nadia Okonkwo

We serve alcohol only to guests aged 21 and over, and we card everyone who looks under 40. Some wines and ciders are fined with egg white or fish products. Ask and we will check the producer's notes for you. See the allergen disclaimer.

Cocktails

built on the herb bed out back

Twelve Mile Old Fashioned

$16

Cask strength Tennessee bourbon, sorghum syrup instead of sugar, and bitters we tincture from black walnut hulls picked in October.

  • Stirred 40 turns over one hand-cut cube
  • Sorghum gives it a darker, grassier finish than demerara
  • Contains tree nuts from the walnut bitters
GFNBar pick

Cider House Sour

$15

Apple brandy, lemon, honey from hives on the Charleston roof, and a float of dry cider from Noble Hard Cider in Waynesville.

  • Shaken hard, no egg white, still foams from the cider
  • Honey changes character three times a year
  • Ask for it long over ice in summer
GF

Garden Bed Gimlet

$14

Gin, lime cordial made with the whole fruit, and whichever soft herb is strongest in the bed that week: mint, shiso or lemon balm.

  • Herb of the week is chalked on the bar
  • Cordial uses whole limes so nothing is wasted
  • The lightest drink on the list
VGGF

Smoked Chili Paloma

$15

Blanco tequila, grapefruit, lime and a syrup made from peppers we smoke and dry ourselves. Salted rim on request.

  • Heat sits at a steady medium, not a dare
  • Peppers come from Rivercane Gardens
  • Half the salt on request, or none at all
VGGF

Muscadine Spritz

$13

Muscadine grape wine from the Yadkin Valley, a bitter aperitif, soda and a wide strip of orange peel.

  • Low alcohol, roughly 8 percent by volume
  • Muscadine is the native Southern grape
  • Good before dinner, poor after it
VGGF

Beer & cider

eight taps, all Southeastern

Burial Beer Co. Pilsner, Asheville NC

$7

Crisp German-style pilsner, 4.8 percent, poured in a 16 ounce glass. Brewed two miles from our Asheville door.

  • The staff drink of choice after a double
  • Pitcher of 60 ounces for $24

Westbrook Gose, Mount Pleasant SC

$8

Salted sour wheat beer, 4 percent, tart enough to cut through the fattier plates on the hearth section.

  • Order it alongside the pork chop
  • Contains wheat and coriander

Noble Dry Hard Cider, Waynesville NC

$9

Bone dry cider pressed from mountain apples, 6.5 percent, with real tannin from the fruit rather than added acid.

  • Naturally gluten free
  • Bottle of 750ml for $28
GFVG

Yazoo Dos Perros Brown Ale, Nashville TN

$8

Malty American brown ale, 3.5 percent, on tap in Nashville only. The right partner for anything from the hardwood grill.

  • Nashville dining room only
  • Session strength, order two

Wine

deliberately short, mostly low intervention

Chenin Blanc, Vouvray, Loire Valley 2022

$14 glass / $54 bottle

Dry, waxy and mineral, with just enough weight for the trout and the cabbage. The single most versatile bottle on our list.

  • Poured in a 6 ounce measure
  • Vegan, no animal fining agents used
VG

Cabernet Franc, Yadkin Valley, North Carolina 2021

$15 glass / $58 bottle

Grown 120 miles from Asheville. Green pepper on the nose, red fruit underneath, tannin light enough to chill for twenty minutes.

  • The closest wine to our own radius
  • Served at cellar temperature, not room
Bar pick

Skin Contact Pinot Gris, Willamette Valley 2022

$16 glass / $62 bottle

Ten days on the skins gives it apricot colour and a grippy finish. Built for the shellfish pot and the onion tart.

  • Unfiltered, so expect some sediment
  • If you have never had an orange wine, start here
VG

Gamay, Beaujolais Villages 2023

$13 glass / $50 bottle

Light, bright and served cold. This is the bottle we open for staff meal, which tells you what we think of it.

  • Works with everything on the garden section
  • Half bottles available for $27
VG

Zero proof

same effort, no alcohol

Blackberry Shrub and Soda

$9

Blackberries macerated in cider vinegar and sugar for a fortnight, then lengthened with soda and a lot of ice.

  • Sharp, not sweet, and genuinely refreshing
  • The shrub base is also sold on the pantry shelf
VGGF

Fennel and Grapefruit Tonic

$10

Fennel seed cordial, fresh grapefruit and a dry tonic water. Bitter enough to work as a genuine aperitif.

  • Contains quinine from the tonic
  • Ask for it without sugar and we adjust the build
VGGF

House Water Kefir, Mint and Cucumber

$8

Lightly fizzy fermented water kefir, second-fermented with mint and cucumber. Trace alcohol under 0.5 percent, as with any live ferment.

  • Brewed in the Asheville basement, bottled weekly
  • Not suitable if you avoid all alcohol entirely
VGGF

Coffee & tea

roasted for us in Asheville

Kraft House Filter Coffee

$5

A washed Colombian and Ethiopian blend roasted for us in small batches. Brewed by the litre and refilled at no charge.

  • Free refills for as long as you are sitting down
  • Whole beans sold at the host stand, 12 ounce bag for $18
VGGF

Garden Tisane, Pot for Two

$9

Whatever we dried at the end of last season: lemon balm, tulsi, chamomile and a little dried apple. Caffeine free.

  • Blend is chalked at the host stand each month
  • Steeps twice, second pot of water is free
VGGF

Pairings without the theatre

Ask any server for a pairing and you get a straight answer at three price points, not a script. If a $13 glass suits your plate better than a $58 bottle, that is what we will tell you.

Chef's Table guests can add a wine and cider pairing for $75 per person, or the zero proof pairing of shrubs, ferments and teas for $45.
