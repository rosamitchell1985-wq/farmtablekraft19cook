Gift cards

A Farm Table Kraft card is worth exactly what you put on it, for as long as it takes to use. No expiry date, no monthly dormancy deduction, no activation charge, and no clause that lets us quietly claim the balance back.

Buy a card

Digital cards send within five minutes of payment. Physical cards are letterpressed on the same kraft stock as our menus and ship free by USPS inside the United States, usually in three to five business days.

No. There is no expiry date printed on the card and none recorded against it in our system. Federal law under the CARD Act requires at least five years, and several states we operate in prohibit expiry on retail gift cards entirely. We simply do not apply one at all, in any state, at any value.

All three restaurants, the private dining room, catering invoices, Chef's Table bookings, first-party online orders and the pantry shelf. The only thing a card cannot be used for is an order placed through a third-party delivery app, because those platforms process payment themselves and never pass us the transaction.

Balances under $10 can be taken as cash change where state law allows, which currently covers all three of our states. Cards are not reloadable and cannot be exchanged for cash above that threshold. If you lose a physical card, email us the purchase confirmation and we will void the old number and issue a replacement at the remaining balance, once, free of charge.

Orders of ten cards or more can be invoiced rather than paid by card, and we will print your own message on the sleeve at no cost. Write to giftcards@farmtablekraft.com with the quantity and denomination. We do not offer a bulk discount, because a $50 card is worth $50 of food and discounting it would just mean charging someone else more.
