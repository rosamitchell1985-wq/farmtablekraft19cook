Menus for groups

Group menus are drawn from the same card as the dining room, narrowed to three choices per course so the kitchen can send 40 plates at the same temperature. You choose the shortlist two weeks out, once we know what the farms will have.

  • Vegan, vegetarian, gluten free and nut free versions of every course, at no extra charge
  • Children's plates at $14 per child for seated events
  • Whole hog or whole lamb service for buyouts, quoted per animal
  • Cake cutting is free, we do not charge a plating fee for a cake you brought

Deposits and cancellation

Private room and buyout bookings take a 25 percent deposit to hold the date. It is fully refundable up to 30 days before the event, half refundable to 14 days, and non-refundable inside 14 days because the food is already bought.
