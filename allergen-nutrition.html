Allergen and nutrition disclaimer

Last updated August 12, 2026. Read this before relying on any allergen or nutrition information on this website or on our menus. It explains what we can promise, what we cannot, and how to give yourself the best chance of a safe meal here.

The critical part

Our kitchens handle wheat, dairy, eggs, tree nuts, peanuts, soy, fish, shellfish and sesame in shared spaces on shared equipment. We cannot guarantee that any dish is free from any allergen. If you have a severe allergy, please speak to a manager before you order so we can tell you honestly whether we can serve you safely that night.

2. The nine major allergens, and where they live in our kitchens

United States law recognises nine major food allergens. All nine are present in at least one of our kitchens.

3. Why we will not promise you an allergen free plate

A single kitchen prepares every dish. Pans, boards, fryers, grills, cloths and hands move between preparations across a service. Flour becomes airborne during the bake and settles across surfaces. Ingredients arrive from suppliers whose own facilities we do not control, and a supplier can change a formulation without telling us.

Any restaurant that tells you a dish is guaranteed allergen free in a shared kitchen is either using the word loosely or is not thinking carefully. We would rather be direct: we will take enormous care, we will change our process for you where we can, and we will still not promise an outcome we cannot control.

4. What we do actively do

  • Allergy information taken at booking or on an order goes to a ticket in the kitchen, not just to the host stand.
  • We keep a written ingredient specification for every dish, updated when a supplier changes.
  • On request we will use a freshly cleaned board, a clean pan, fresh oil and a separate service cloth for your plate.
  • The dish will be run by a manager or the chef before it leaves the pass.
  • Where we judge that we cannot serve you safely, we will say so plainly rather than take the risk with your health.
  • Servers are trained to bring the manager rather than guess. If a server ever answers an allergy question with a guess, tell us and we will treat it seriously.

5. Nutrition information

We do not publish calorie counts or a full nutritional breakdown for our dishes, and we want to be clear about why. Our menu changes weekly around what farms deliver, portion weights vary because vegetables are not uniform, and a plate cooked to order is not a standardised industrial product. Numbers produced under those conditions would be an estimate presented with false precision.

What we will do instead, at any time, is tell you exactly what is in a dish: every ingredient, the fats used, the cooking method and the approximate portion size. Ask a server or email hello@farmtablekraft.com in advance and we will send you the current specification for any dish on the menu.

Nothing on this website is medical or dietary advice. If you are managing a medical condition through your diet, please rely on your clinician or dietitian, and use our ingredient information as raw material for that conversation rather than as guidance in itself.

6. Alcohol

Some wines, ciders and beers are fined using egg white, casein, gelatin or isinglass, which is derived from fish. Producers are not required to disclose this on a US label. We hold producer notes for most of our list and will check for you before you order. Trace alcohol is present in our water kefir, as it is in any live ferment.

7. General website disclaimer

The content on this website is provided for general information about our restaurants. We work to keep menus, prices, hours and ingredient details accurate and current, and the food menu changes weekly, which means the card in the restaurant on any given night may differ from the version published here. Where they differ, the printed card in the restaurant is authoritative.

This site contains no affiliate links and we receive no commission from any outbound link. Clearly labelled advertising appears on some pages; advertisements are supplied by third parties and are not endorsements by us. Nothing on this site is professional, medical, legal or financial advice.

8. Talk to us before you visit

The single most useful thing you can do is tell us in advance. Add the detail to the notes field when you book, or call the restaurant. A kitchen that knows at 3:00 PM can plan properly. A kitchen that finds out at 8:15 PM in the middle of a full service is working with far less room.
