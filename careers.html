Wages printed, not negotiated in the dark

Work with us

Every role below shows its wage, its schedule and its actual demands before you apply. We think an applicant should not have to sit through two interviews to find out what a job pays.

What the job comes with

Pay

Front of house from $18 per hour before tips. Kitchen from $21. No tip credit taken against wages, in any state.

Health

Medical, dental and vision from 20 hours a week, with the company covering 70 percent of the employee premium.

Time

Two consecutive days off, published two weeks ahead. Five annual company closure days, paid.

Ownership

A 12 percent employee ownership pool. Shares vest after three years of continuous service.

Open roles

Six positions across the three restaurants, current as of this month. If nothing here fits, send us a note anyway, because turnover is real and we keep good applications on file for six months.

Kitchen

Asheville and Nashville

Line Cook, Asheville

$21 to $25 per hour

Full time, four or five shifts a week, hearth or garde manger station depending on where you are strongest.

  • Two years of professional kitchen experience preferred, not required
  • Shifts run 2:00 PM to 11:00 PM, two consecutive days off
  • You will learn butchery whether or not you asked to

Baker, Asheville

$23 to $26 per hour

Full time, five shifts. Bread for all three restaurants is baked here, which means the day starts at 3:40 AM and ends at noon.

  • Sourdough experience required, we will not teach it from scratch in this role
  • Shift differential of $2 per hour for the early start
  • Home by lunch, every day

Sous Chef, Nashville

$62,000 to $70,000 per year

Salaried, running the pass four nights a week and the hardwood grill programme. Reports to the head chef, works with Marisol on the weekly menu.

  • Live fire cooking experience essential
  • Ordering and stock responsibility for the meat programme
  • Relocation support of up to $3,000 available

Front of house and support

all three locations

Server, Charleston

$18 per hour plus tips

Part time or full time. Tips are pooled across the whole shift including kitchen, and average an additional $12 to $19 per hour across a month.

  • No experience needed, we train the menu properly
  • You will be expected to learn where every ingredient comes from
  • Courtyard shifts March to November

Bartender, Nashville

$19 per hour plus tips

Full time, four shifts including both weekend nights. Our largest bar, with a zero proof list that gets ordered as often as the cocktails.

  • Two years behind a bar required
  • You will help build the seasonal list, not just pour it
  • Tennessee alcohol server permit required within 30 days

Produce Driver and Buyer's Assistant, Asheville

$20 per hour

Three mornings a week, 5:30 AM to 11:30 AM. You drive the Transit, collect from up to nine farms, and check every crate before it goes on the truck.

  • Clean driving licence required, we insure the vehicle
  • Physical work, crates run to 40 pounds
  • The best way into this company if you want to learn sourcing
