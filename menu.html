The menu

Twenty-six dishes across six courses. Prices are in US dollars, exclude sales tax, and carry no automatic service charge. Ingredient drawings sit beside every dish so you can see the plant, root or animal the plate is built on.

The Chef's Table tasting

Eight seats face the pass. Marisol and the sous chefs cook and plate in front of you, and explain what came in that morning and why it ended up on your plate in that particular form.

$145 per guest for nine to eleven courses, roughly two and a half hours. Wine and cider pairing is an extra $75 per guest, or a non-alcoholic pairing of shrubs, teas and ferments for $45.

  • Wednesday to Saturday, one seating at 6:30 PM
  • Whole table books together, no split seatings
  • Dietary needs handled if you tell us 72 hours ahead
  • Full prepayment at booking, refundable up to 72 hours before

Allergies and dietary needs

Tell your server before you order, not after. Our kitchen can adapt most dishes, and where it cannot, we will say so plainly rather than guess. Detailed ingredient information is on the allergen and nutrition page.

What the price includes

Menu prices are the full food price. Sales tax is added at the applicable state and local rate. We add no automatic service charge, no kitchen appreciation fee and no card surcharge. Gratuity is entirely your call.
