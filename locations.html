Three rooms, three radiuses

Locations and hours

Each restaurant buys inside its own twelve mile circle, so the menus rhyme without repeating. Below are the full hours, how to park, how to arrive without a car, and exactly what the access is like at each door.

Asheville, North Carolina

The original room, opened in 2014 with 38 seats and now holding 78. Open kitchen along the back wall, hardwood grill, and the private Rhododendron Room up the stair. This is where the whole-animal butchery happens for all three restaurants.

42 Biltmore Avenue
Asheville, NC 28801, United States
Phone: +1 (828) 555-0142
Email: asheville@farmtablekraft.com
Opening hours
MondayClosed
Tuesday to Thursday11:30 AM to 9:00 PM
Friday and Saturday11:30 AM to 10:00 PM
Sunday10:00 AM to 8:00 PM

Getting here and getting in

  • Two reserved takeout bays directly outside on Biltmore Avenue
  • Rankin Avenue garage is a four minute walk, roughly $2 per hour
  • ART bus routes S3 and S6 stop within one block
  • Step-free entrance from the sidewalk, no lip at the door
  • Accessible restroom on the ground floor with a 34 inch doorway
  • The upstairs private room has stair access only, so we move private bookings downstairs on request

Charleston, South Carolina

Our second room, opened 2019 in a former dry-goods store on King Street. Ninety-six seats, a walled courtyard that opens in March and closes in November, and a Lowcountry-leaning menu built around the shrimp boats out of Shem Creek.

218 King Street
Charleston, SC 29401, United States
Phone: +1 (843) 555-0188
Email: charleston@farmtablekraft.com
Opening hours
Monday to Thursday11:30 AM to 9:30 PM
Friday and Saturday11:30 AM to 10:30 PM
Sunday10:00 AM to 9:00 PM
Courtyard serviceMarch through November, weather permitting

Getting here and getting in

  • Queen Street garage is two blocks south, $2.50 per hour
  • Metered street parking on King Street is free after 6:00 PM
  • CARTA route 20 and the free DASH trolley both stop nearby
  • Step-free entrance through the main door, threshold under half an inch
  • Two accessible restrooms, both with grab rails and a turning circle
  • Courtyard is reached over flagstones, which can be uneven after rain

Nashville, Tennessee

Opened 2023 in Germantown. The biggest bar of the three, a hardwood grill that runs from open to close, and the only kitchen where the whole hog shoulder is smoked on site rather than trucked in from Asheville.

1201 5th Avenue North

Getting here and getting in

  • Free surface lot behind the building with 22 spaces, two accessible
  • WeGo route 22 stops at 5th and Monroe, one block away
  • Ramped entrance at the rear, level entrance at the front
  • Bar seating is 42 inches high, low tables available on request
  • Accessible restroom with an automatic door
  • Induction hearing loop at the host stand and the bar

Days we are closed

Every restaurant closes on the same five days a year so the whole company can take them together, and each room takes a weekly rest day as listed above.

Annual closures across all locations
ClosureDatesNotes
New Year's DayJanuary 1All three restaurants closed
Deep clean weekFirst full week of FebruaryKitchens stripped and re-seasoned
Independence DayJuly 4Closed, staff paid as normal
ThanksgivingFourth Thursday of NovemberClosed, catering deliveries only
ChristmasDecember 24 evening to December 26Closed, gift cards still work online

Storm closures happen in Asheville and Charleston. When they do, we post on the phone line greeting first and social media second. Reservations affected by a closure are refunded in full without you having to ask.
