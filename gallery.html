About these photographs

Every image is shot in the light the room actually has. We do not use food stylists, glycerine, motor oil in place of syrup, or any of the other tricks that make restaurant photography look nothing like the plate you get. If a dish photographs badly, that is our problem to solve in the kitchen rather than in the edit.

If you would like to use any of these images for press, write to press@farmtablekraft.com and we will send high resolution files with the correct photographer credit.
