Accessibility statement

Last updated August 12, 2026. We aim to meet Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.1 at Level AA across this website, and to make all three restaurants usable by every guest who walks, rolls or is guided through the door.

1. Our commitment

Accessibility is not a compliance exercise we did once. It is part of how this site is built and how the restaurants are run. We take the position that a guest should not have to ask for special treatment to read a menu, book a table or get to a restroom.

This site targets conformance with WCAG 2.1 Level AA. We consider it substantially conformant, with the known exceptions listed in section 4.

2. What is in place on this website

  • Semantic structure. Every page uses proper landmarks: a banner, a navigation region, a main region and a footer, with headings in a logical order and no levels skipped.
  • Skip link. The first thing keyboard focus reaches on every page is a "Skip to main content" link.
  • Keyboard operation. Every control, including the mobile menu, the dropdown menus, the FAQ accordions, the gallery filter and the cookie banner, works with a keyboard alone. Escape closes open menus.
  • Visible focus. A three pixel focus ring in our accent colour appears on every focusable element. We have not removed default focus styling anywhere.
  • Contrast. Body text is checked to at least 4.5 to 1 against its background, and large text and interface components to at least 3 to 1. Our body ink on kraft paper measures approximately 7 to 1.
  • Images. Every photograph carries descriptive alternative text. Decorative drawings are marked so screen readers skip them.
  • Forms. Every field has a visible label, not a placeholder standing in for one. Errors appear next to the field they belong to, are announced to assistive technology, and describe how to fix the problem rather than simply saying the entry is invalid.
  • Motion. Animations respect the operating system "reduce motion" setting and collapse to no movement at all when it is on. Nothing on this site flashes, auto-plays audio or moves without you asking.
  • Zoom and reflow. The layout works at 320 pixels wide and at 400 percent browser zoom without horizontal scrolling or lost content. Text can be resized to 200 percent without breaking.
  • Touch targets. Interactive controls are at least 44 by 44 pixels with clear spacing between them.
  • No traps. No popup blocks the page on arrival. The cookie banner sits at the bottom, is reachable by keyboard, and does not prevent you reading the page underneath.

3. Assistive technology we test with

Pages are checked with VoiceOver on macOS and iOS, NVDA on Windows with Firefox, keyboard-only navigation in Chrome, and browser zoom to 400 percent. We test at 360, 768, 1024, 1440 and 1920 pixels wide.

4. Known limitations, stated honestly

These are the things we know are not yet right. We do not think it is helpful to publish a statement that claims perfection.

  • Some longer data tables, such as the example event quote, require horizontal scrolling on very narrow screens. The table scrolls inside its own container rather than pushing the page sideways, but it is still less comfortable than we would like.
  • PDF menus supplied to third-party listing sites are not fully tagged for screen readers. The HTML menu on this site is the accessible source of truth and is always current.
  • Third-party delivery platforms we link to have their own accessibility, which we do not control. If one of them creates a barrier for you, tell us and we will take your order directly instead.
  • The Asheville private dining room is upstairs with stair access only. We move any private booking to a ground floor configuration on request, at no additional cost, and we would rather you asked than assumed.

6. Tell us about a barrier

If something on this site or in a restaurant does not work for you, we want to hear about it and we will treat it as a defect rather than a complaint.

7. Formal approach and review

This statement was prepared following a self-evaluation of the site against WCAG 2.1 Level AA, carried out in August 2026. We review it at least twice a year and whenever we ship a substantial change to the site. Farm Table Kraft LLC also works to meet its obligations under the Americans with Disabilities Act in its physical premises.
