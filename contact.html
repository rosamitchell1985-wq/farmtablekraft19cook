When you will hear back

  • Reservation questions: same day during opening hours
  • General messages through this form: one business day
  • Event and catering enquiries: one business day, with an itemised quote
  • Privacy and data requests: within 45 days, as required under California law, and usually much sooner
  • Job applications: four business days, whether or not we are moving forward

If you have written to us and not had a reply inside those windows, something went wrong. Call the main line and say so.
